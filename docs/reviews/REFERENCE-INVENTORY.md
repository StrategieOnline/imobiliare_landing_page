## Hero proportions (what I compare our screenshots against)

- Scroll share in the recording: ch.0+split ≈ 4%, rise ≈ 13%, seam ≈ 11%, loggia ≈ 17%, interior ≈ 21%, card ≈ 15% of the hero time. This is close to the spec's 12/18/18/16/18/18 split. **The canvas→card is the last ~6–7% of the hero.**
- Headline cap height ≈ 7–8% of the viewport height. Left edge ≈ 5% of the viewport width. The baseline of the last headline line sits at ≈ 80% of the viewport height. The body is 2 lines at ~14 px under it.
- Wordmark at rest: ≈ 85% of the viewport width, baseline at ≈ 80% of the viewport height, cream with no stroke.
- Chapter list: right side, vertically centred under the cursor ring, mono ~9–10 px, the active row brighter with a longer dash.
- Corner crop marks: top-left (with the city tag), top-right (with the active chapter tag), bottom-left (with the TC).
- Nav over the hero: transparent. The pill is gold-soft in early chapters and turns pale/inverted over bright plates, which is consistent with `mix-blend-mode: difference`. The nav is **not visible** from the manifesto onward in the reference. Our spec instead keeps it with difference/solid ink. **The spec wins.**

## Component inventory (what we must have an equivalent of)

1. Nav: wordmark, place tag, 4 indexed mono links (they light up progressively), pill CTA.
2. Hero overlays: crop marks ×3, running TC, `SCROLL TO ENTER` hint, chapter index above each headline, right chapter list, active-chapter tag top-right.
3. Cursor ring: lerped. A label (`SCROLL`) in the hero, a dot in content, a filled disc over images/links.
4. Text motion: per-line mask reveal (from below); blur + fade exit; word-by-word brightness scrub; digit scramble; mono character scramble (bonus); smeared headline.
5. 3D/plate motion: wordmark letter fly-through, floor-by-floor construction rise, orbit + dolly to the seam, zoom-blur push into the loggia, through-the-doors threshold, interior dolly with parallax, canvas→card inset.
6. Sections: manifesto + 4 stats, marquee (filled/outlined, ✦), pinned horizontal 3-card gallery (outlined numerals, image clip/parallax), indexed list with hover preview, materials image with slow scale, location with image + coordinates + distance table + map link, booking form (2 columns), footer with giant clipped wordmark.
