Hence 6/10: the structure is excellent and the look is not yet there.

### Art direction

- **Windows.** I agree with the lead, and it needs a fix.
  - **Too much lit, all at the same brightness:** about 30% of panels are lit at **exactly the same emissive** (one colour, strength 1.2, full panel, edge to edge). The result is a Tetris/barcode pattern rather than a lived-in tower.
  - **Matte glass:** the dark panels render as matte black, because the render world has no reflections.
  - **Reference and our photo plates:** the tower at dusk reads as **dark bronze glass reflecting the sky, with warm accents** (mostly the loggias and a few rooms). Here the façade fights the seam for attention.
- **Seam.**
  - **What the reference does:** the diagonal is carried **by the loggias themselves** (the stack leans, t_2.0–3.0), with stone framing them.
  - **What the model does:** the diagonal is only the left edge of a large **blank stone wedge**, up to 14 m of flat stone on the front face at the base. The loggias stay in a fixed vertical column at the corner.
  - **Why it matters in the hero:** at chapter 2's ≥40%-of-frame close-up, that wedge will be a big flat salmon plane.
  - **Consistency:** it also doesn't match MEDIA's `tower-exterior` / `location` / `og` (a narrow corner band filled by loggias, with a mid-height shift), so the hero tower and the section photos will read as two buildings.
- **Loggia close-up.** The geometry is good: stone returns, cove soffit, sliding-door frames. But:
  - The back wall is one flat emissive plane.
  - The vertical-bar railing looks utilitarian next to MEDIA's frameless glass balustrades.
  - All 24 loggias are lit identically, so they read as a perfect ladder.
- **Crown.** The lantern's thin lit strips read as glyphs (`ılılı`) at distance.

### Fixes (numbered, for the team)

1. **major · `blender/build_tower.py:317-324` (`facade_ring`) and `:354` (`glow_share=0.30`) · Lit windows.** Rework them so the façade reads as homes, not pixels. Keep the single `M_WindowGlow` material name, and do it with data:
   - **Occupancy by apartment, not by random runs.** 46 homes on 24 floors is about 2 apartments per floor, and 1 per floor on 22–23. Per floor, decide each apartment "home / away" (~50% home at dusk). In a home apartment, light 1–2 *rooms* of 2–4 adjacent panels. Overall lit share on the tower floors should be **~12–18%** (now 30%). Keep the podium and crown rules separate.
   - **Varied warmth and intensity** through an **`emissiveTexture` atlas** on `M_WindowGlow`: one 256×512 JPEG (≤60 KB) with 8 "room cards".
     - The cards are warm 2700 K to neutral 3300 K, bright to dim (0.25–1.0).
     - Each card has a vertical gradient: brighter ceiling/cove band, darker sill.
     - A few cards have sheer curtains or furniture silhouettes, which gives the cheap interior-depth fake.
     - Each lit panel's UVs point at one card, chosen per room so a room's panels match. `emissiveFactor` stays white × strength. This is plain glTF core, so three.js renders it with no shader work.
   - **Loggias follow occupancy:** a home apartment gets a lit loggia back wall, an away one gets a dim card. No more uniform ladder.
   - **Fixed looks like:** in `tower_34.png` at 50% size, the façade is mostly dark reflective bronze with irregular, clustered, varied warm rooms. Nobody can see a grid.
2. **major · `blender/build_tower.py` seam constants (`SEAM_FRONT`, `seam_x()` `:63`, `wrap_y()` `:71`, `seam_storey()` `:422`) · The blank stone wedge.** Make the stone the *frame of the loggias*, not a blank wedge.
   - **Stone width:** ≈ loggia width + 1.2–1.5 m piers each side (≈ 8 m), constant up the height.
   - **The lean:** move the loggia column with the diagonal. Each storey's `Loggia_XX` x shifts by Δ, so the stack leans along the front face, as in the reference. Alternatively, a deliberate shift over 3–4 storeys at mid-height, matching MEDIA's photos.
   - **Stone surface:** whatever stone remains gets panel coursing (vertical joints ~1.2–1.5 m, the storey joints kept), slight per-panel tone variation (two or three stone UV offsets), and the texture at ~1.5 m per repeat.
   - **Fixed looks like:** in `seam_closeup.png` no flat stone area wider than ~2 m remains, and the eye reads "stacked rooms carved into stone".
   - **Recommendation to the lead:** make the **3D the canonical silhouette**, and have MEDIA regenerate `tower-exterior` → `location` → `og` with the fixed `tower_34.png` as the image reference (SPEC §4 anticipates this). That makes the hero tower and the section photos one building. It also covers MEDIA finding 6: 24 storeys, darker glass.
3. **minor · render rig (`setup_render_world()` `:591`) · Render with reflections.**
   - **What's wrong:** without an environment the 0.6-metal glass renders matte black, so the renders under-sell the model and mislead MEDIA if used as references.
   - **Fixed looks like:** a dusk sky texture or HDRI as the world (similar to what HERO will feed PMREM), a second still from the chapter-1 camera position (far, in the skyline, low sun back-right to match `skyline-dusk`), and a still from the chapter-2 camera distance.
4. **minor · `build_loggia()` `:371` · Balustrade.** Replace the vertical-bar railing with a frameless glass balustrade with a slim bronze cap (one quad + one box), to match MEDIA's loggias and save triangles. Optionally put a 2–3 card interior fake on the back wall (same atlas as fix 1).
5. **minor · `build_crown()` `:486` · Lantern.** Make the lit strips read as a continuous softly lit band: wider panels, a lower strength, and the fins in front. Not separate slivers.
6. **minor · bare-frame storeys.** With `Glass_XX` hidden, a storey is an empty box under its lid.
   - **Fixed looks like:** a few slim columns and a core block per storey in `Floor_XX` (low tris, `M_Frame` or a concrete grey). Then the §7.1 construction look reads as structure. Check it in a still with the top 3 `Glass_XX` hidden.
7. **minor · `docs/HANDOFF-3D.md` and `docs/ASSETS.md` · Accuracy.**
   - Say "the exported GLBs and texture are byte-identical between runs; renders and the .blend differ only in noise and metadata".
   - Note that the stats line `ÎNĂLȚIME 96 m` sits between the roof (92.4) and the crown (97.8). That's fine, but state which height marketing uses.
